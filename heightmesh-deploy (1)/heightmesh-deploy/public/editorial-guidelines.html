Editorial Guidelines

Last updated: September 26, 2026

These Editorial Guidelines describe how the Height Comparison Tool team researches, writes, verifies, and maintains all content on this site — including height data, the mid-parental formula calculator, growth exercise instructions, and nutritional guidance.

1. Sourcing & Verification

  • Celebrity heights: Sourced from Wikipedia, which cites primary sources (interviews, official biographies, sports league records). We update our database quarterly.
  • Mid-parental formula: Sourced from J.M. Tanner, H. Goldstein, and P.H. Whitehouse (1970), "Standards for Children's Height at Ages 2–9 Years Allowing for Height of Parents," Archives of Disease in Childhood.
  • Exercise instructions: Adapted from the American Academy of Pediatrics physical activity guidelines and peer-reviewed physiotherapy literature.
  • Conversion factors: 1 inch = 2.54 cm (international inch), 1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 cm — internationally standardized by the yard-and-pound agreement of 1959.

2. Medical Review

All content involving medical formulas, growth prediction, or posture guidance is reviewed by a volunteer reviewer with background in pediatric endocrinology or physiotherapy. The reviewer verifies:

  • Accuracy of the formula and its stated standard deviation
  • Appropriateness of the displayed range (±10 cm for mid-parental)
  • Clarity of the medical disclaimer
  • Safety of exercise instructions and inclusion of contraindications

3. Update Frequency

  • Celebrity database: Quarterly (every 3 months)
  • Exercise library: Annually, or as new evidence emerges
  • Calculator formula: Updated only when consensus changes in the pediatric literature
  • Legal pages (Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer): Annually or upon regulatory changes

4. Corrections Policy

If you spot an error, please contact support@heightcomparison.app. We investigate all reports within 5 business days and publish corrections with a dated "Last updated" note on the affected page.

5. Author Byline

Every long-form article and the calculator itself display an author byline ("Height Comparison Tool Team") and a "Last updated" date. The full author and reviewer list is available on the About page.

6. Conflict of Interest

The Height Comparison Tool team has no financial relationships with supplement brands, growth hormone manufacturers, or fitness equipment vendors. We do not accept sponsored content or paid placements. Any future advertising (e.g., Google AdSense) will be clearly labeled and segregated from editorial content.

7. AI & Automation Disclosure

Source code is written by human engineers. Some long-form text on the site (e.g., exercise benefit descriptions) may be drafted with AI assistance and then reviewed, edited, and verified by a human editor. We never publish AI-generated medical claims without verification against peer-reviewed sources.
