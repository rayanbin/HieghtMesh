Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

The Height Comparison Tool ("we", "us", or "our") operates entirely client-side in your web browser. This Privacy Policy explains what data is — and is not — collected when you use our visual height comparison, child height calculator, growth exercises, and celebrity height search features.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All characters you add to the comparison chart, all calculator inputs (parents' heights, lifestyle quiz answers), and all preferences (color selections, unit toggles) are stored only in your browser's memory (JavaScript state) and are permanently lost the moment you close or refresh the page.

2. Wikipedia API Requests

When you use the celebrity search feature, your search query is sent directly from your browser to the Wikipedia REST API (en.wikipedia.org) to retrieve celebrity names, profile images, and descriptions. This request is governed by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy. We do not proxy or intercept these requests.

3. Cookies

This site does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. The only browser storage we use is localStorage for the cookie consent banner preference (whether you accepted or dismissed the GDPR/CCPA notice). No identifiers, fingerprinting, or session IDs are stored.

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect aggregate, anonymized traffic statistics (page views, country-level geography, device type). GA4 is configured with IP anonymization and does not track individual user behavior across sessions. You can opt out by using browser extensions like Privacy Badger or by enabling Do Not Track in your browser.

5. Third-Party Ad Networks

If we display ads in the future via Google AdSense, those ads may use cookies to serve relevant content. We will update this policy and display a GDPR/CCPA consent banner before enabling any ad network.

6. Children's Privacy

The Child Potential Height Calculator is a tool for parents and guardians to estimate a child's adult height. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected (answer: none)
  • Request deletion of personal information (not applicable — nothing is stored server-side)
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we never sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@heightcomparison.app.
